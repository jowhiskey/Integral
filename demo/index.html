Integral editor demo — session 2 (render + caret + click + keyboard)

Click a formula to focus it, then: type (5+3*2, (a+b)), move with the arrow keys (try Down/Left/Left/Up inside the x+y over c+d fraction — vertical positional memory), undo with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z. The readout under each formula shows the caret and the current envelope.
