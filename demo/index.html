Integral editor demo — session 5 (visual-test case browser)

The case list is the visual-test catalog (demo/cases.ts), grouped by category with stable IDs. Click a case to load its seed (formula + starting caret) into the case editor; click the active case again to reset to the seed. Instruction cases (marked keys) seed the empty formula — the “type instruction” button runs the key sequence through the real keyboard path. In the editor: type, move with the arrow keys, Backspace / Ctrl+Backspace, undo/redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z), and click to place the caret. The readout under the case editor shows the caret and the current envelope. The scratchpad at the bottom is an always-free empty editor.
