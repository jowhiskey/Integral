Integral editor demo — visual-test case browser + usability-test logger

about this page (dev-only demo, not part of the published package)

The case list below is the visual-test catalog (demo/cases.ts), grouped by category with stable IDs. Click a case to load its seed (formula + starting caret) into the case editor; click the active case again to reset to the seed. Instruction cases (marked keys) seed the empty formula — the “type instruction” button runs the key sequence through the real keyboard path. In the editor: type, move with the arrow keys, Backspace / Ctrl+Backspace, undo/redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z), and click to place the caret. The panel right of the editor is the usability-test logger: press Record, act in the editor, Stop — every key, click, and case load is captured as one diff entry (baseline first); expand an entry to re-render the exact state at that step and pin a comment. Recordings persist in localStorage; export-one / export-all download text-only JSON bug files for a fix session. The paint-geometry toggle adds the caret overlay rect and anchor element rect to entries.
